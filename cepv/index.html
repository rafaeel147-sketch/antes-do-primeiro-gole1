What the system does

The current MVP separates a general baseline run from a specialist execution, records required checks, findings, evidence, blocked/failed checks and coverage, and supports blinded comparison plus human adjudication.

The verification layer is conceptually separable from the underlying model provider, allowing a future buyer to adapt it to different model/API environments.

Current technical assets

  • Python-based engine components
  • Versioned specialist profile methodology
  • Execution-contract structure
  • Evidence and evaluation schemas
  • Browser adjudication UI
  • Regression-tested workflows

Real-world evidence

A supplemental real-world editorial case has been documented in which a later specialist review identified multiple textual, factual/institutional, authorship/transparency, layout, metadata, rights and narrative-integrity issues that remained after an earlier review.

Buyer opportunity

A buyer with product and engineering capability could develop CEPV into a commercial document-QA product, an internal enterprise verification layer, a white-label QA service or a broader framework for specialist AI evaluation workflows.

Validation disclosure: CEPV is not being presented as scientifically proven to outperform a general-purpose model. Its own methodology requires an independent human review, adjudication, a frozen revised ground truth and a formal isolated A/B run before such a claim is made. This page describes the current MVP, methodology and accumulated evidence only.
