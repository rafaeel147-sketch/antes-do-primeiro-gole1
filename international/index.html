A human-led process

Authorship and accountability matter.

Rafael conceives and directs the initiatives, coordinates AI-assisted workflows, reviews outputs and decides what is ready to share. AI tools are disclosed as collaborators in production—not substituted for lived experience, evidence or independent evaluation.

Workflow: Idea → scoped task → specialist roles → checks → documented evidence → human review → revised output.
Research integrity: Community reception, acceptability, usability and proven effectiveness are different outcomes. This portfolio does not claim clinical efficacy, institutional endorsement or official integration with Brazil's public health system.
