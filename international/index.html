Why independent support matters

In Brazil, many formal funding opportunities are designed for degree-holding researchers or registered organizations. I do not currently hold a university degree or operate through an NGO/OSC. This limits access to some calls, but does not define my capacity to create, organize and deliver work. I am therefore also seeking modest, transparent individual support—such as a stipend or fellowship—to cover basic living expenses and protected time for research, documentation and prototype development.

Any support would be discussed with clear scope, reporting, applicable eligibility rules and mutually agreed milestones. This is a request for partnership, not a claim of institutional recognition or guaranteed eligibility.

No funding or institutional endorsement is implied. Project budgets, milestones, intellectual-property terms and accountability arrangements can be defined with prospective partners.

A human-led process

Authorship and accountability matter.

Rafael conceives and directs the initiatives, coordinates AI-assisted workflows, reviews outputs and decides what is ready to share. AI tools are disclosed as collaborators in production—not substituted for lived experience, evidence or independent evaluation.

Workflow: Idea → scoped task → specialist roles → checks → documented evidence → human review → revised output.
Research integrity: Community reception, acceptability, usability and proven effectiveness are different outcomes. This portfolio does not claim clinical efficacy, institutional endorsement or official integration with Brazil's public health system.
