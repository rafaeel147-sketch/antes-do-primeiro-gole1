Meet the AI Companions

A visual language for specialized AI roles, quality checks, evidence and accountable human direction.

Conceived and coordinated by Rafael Leandro de Carvalho. The characters are symbolic workflow roles, not autonomous people, clinicians or a certified AI system.

Meet the companions ↓

The companions and their checks

🐒 Macaco — The Builder

Turns ideas into traceable experiments, tasks and practical tests.

Check: Do not confuse prototypes with verified outcomes.

🐔 Galinha à Moral — The Challenger

Questions ethical risks, assumptions and overstated conclusions.

Check: Do not treat suspicion as evidence.

🦜 Papagaio — The Communicator

Converts technical complexity into accessible language.

Check: Preserve nuance and avoid oversimplification.

🦉 Coruja Boss — The Coordinator

Aligns tasks, standards, decisions and review checkpoints.

Check: Human approval remains decisive.

🔎 Sherlock — The Investigator

Traces sources, gaps and contradictions.

Check: Separate verified facts from hypotheses.

🎨 Da Vinci — The Visual Interpreter

Turns validated ideas into coherent visual communication.

Check: Do not depict invented scenes as real evidence.

🐍 Jéssica — The Antidote Lens

Uses the poison-and-antidote metaphor to examine risks and responses.

Check: Metaphor is not clinical advice.

From an idea to a reviewed capability

Specialist profile → execution contract → checks → evidence → human validation → evaluation → validated capability. Each step requires documented criteria; the final term describes a target process, not a claim of independent certification.

Research and collaboration

We welcome independent evaluation, accessible educational-content research, responsible-AI review and support for practical prototypes. Experimental materials require external testing before effectiveness claims.
